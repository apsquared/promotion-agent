## What is deliberately a coaching heuristic

The suggested audit sample, weekly review, 30-day horizon, time-budget examples, limited active experiments and task records are design choices for a manageable learning loop. They are not claims that X rewards those schedules. The user's own results should refine them.

Followers gained per reply is an association across a period with multiple activities. A paid boost coinciding with growth does not establish an acquisition cost. For either claim, require appropriate attribution or call the uncertainty out. Prefer retained audience relevance and the user's chosen outcome over undifferentiated counts.

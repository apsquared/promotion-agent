# Find and qualify promotion opportunities

Use this procedure for directory listings, backlinks, relevant communities, editorial contributions and other placements for one selected product. Follow [the shared workflow](../../WORKFLOW.md) for product discovery, privacy, workspace boundaries, authorization and task tracking. Run qualification under activity `directory`; use existing categories for subsequent submission, engagement, outreach or content tasks. This procedure also applies before preparing any directory/backlink/profile/launch placement kit.

## Admission rule

Recommend **only high-quality sites with a verified current opportunity and evidence supporting referral traffic, SEO value, or both for the selected product**. All three gates below must pass. A famous domain, a working homepage, an old submission, a search snippet, a third-party catalog entry, a high DR/DA number or an advertised dofollow link is insufficient. Unknown is a failed admission gate until verified. Return fewer results, including zero, instead of relaxing the standard.

Separate observed facts from estimates and reasoned expectations. Prospective traffic and ranking gains cannot be guaranteed; call measured results measured, and explain the evidence for expected value. Do not invent minimum traffic/authority numbers or buy access to metrics.

## Scope and discovery

- Resolve the product, audience, destination and goal from its selected facts and the user's request. Ask only for essential missing context. Read its link tracker, prior reports, tasks and skipped/snoozed dispositions before researching.
- Use the current session's search, fetch and browser tools. Search with public product/category terms. [Source notes](SOURCES.md) provide discovery leads and primary guidance; they contain no approved destinations. Do not bulk-import a backlink list or assume a prior catalog entry remains qualified.
- Classify each candidate by the actual opportunity: reviewed directory, product review/comparison, editorial pitch, community contribution, publishing platform or eligible business profile. A publisher's homepage is not a guest-post invitation; a hosting platform is not an earned editorial placement.
- Deduplicate the actual opportunity using the canonical destination and submission route, following redirects and removing tracking aliases. Preserve materially different communities or editorial desks on the same platform; check existing product listings before proposing another. Keep rejection reasons so the next run does not rediscover the same poor fits.

## Gate 1 — site quality and relevance

Open the live site, the relevant category/community/publication and at least one recent comparable placement. Require evidence of a genuine audience matching the product and substantive, maintained content with credible editorial selection or moderation. Record the specific observations and URLs, including visible dates where available. Site-wide reputation does not establish the quality of an individual profile, subdomain, category or thread.

Reject link farms, private blog networks, bulk auto-accept directories, indiscriminate unrelated listings, abandoned/spam-filled communities, manufactured profiles and pages created chiefly to host backlinks. Reject deceptive sites, required manipulative link exchanges, paid ranking-credit offers and optimized-anchor schemes. A fee or a reciprocal badge alone is not proof of quality or SEO benefit; a legitimate sponsorship may qualify only through evidenced traffic value and appropriate link qualification, with spending left to a separately authorized action.

## Gate 2 — evidence of traffic or SEO value

At least one route must pass; document which one and why. Do not let several weak signals add up to a pass.

| Route | Evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Referral traffic | Evidence that relevant people use the specific surface: attributable referral/conversion history supplied for this product, recent substantive activity by relevant participants, or current discoverability/distribution supported by observed search results or dated audience data. Explain how people will find the proposed placement and reach the product through a working link or explicit discovery path. A platform's total visitors, follower count or the publisher's unsupported traffic claim alone does not qualify it. Mark estimates and publisher-reported numbers as such. |
| SEO | A relevant, editorially selected placement with a credible path to an earned contextual link. Inspect a comparable current placement: public accessibility, crawlability/indexability signals, evidence of search indexing, actual outbound destination/redirects and link attributes. Require evidence that the intended link type can support this route; a profile on a strong domain or a claimed dofollow label alone cannot. Record missing checks explicitly and withhold SEO qualification if the required evidence is unavailable. |

Use the existing [keyless domain lookup](../domain-rank.md) only when authority or popularity estimates would help a decision; metrics are supporting evidence, never an admission rule. Do not transfer a platform's authority/traffic to a user's page or equate DR with Google's judgment.

For SEO, distinguish a comparable page's observed indexing from the future placement's unknown indexing. A successful fetch or the absence of `noindex` does not prove indexing. Inspect robots directives and response headers where available; report tool limits. Check current primary search-engine guidance linked in SOURCES.md when making a policy-dependent judgment. `nofollow`, `ugc` and `sponsored` links do not establish ranking credit; they can still qualify through the traffic route. An ordinary unqualified link also guarantees no ranking improvement. Avoid low-value syndication, forum/profile spam and throwaway hosted pages.

## Gate 3 — verify the specific opportunity now

Perform these checks in the current research session before shortlisting; cached reports and old approvals cannot substitute. Use the original live destination and its official instructions. A search result or archived page may identify a lead but cannot establish present availability.

1. Follow the site's visible submission, contribution or participation route. Open the actual form, guidelines or official invitation without sending, registering or spending. Confirm the route is for this placement type and is open; check dates, deadlines, closed queues and paid-only changes. A generic contact page is insufficient evidence that guest pitches are accepted.
2. Match the selected product against niche, geography, business model, launch date, ownership, contributor qualifications and any other eligibility rules. Do not fabricate a local address, credentials, membership or review to fit. Confirm an appropriate category and whether an existing listing requires an update instead.
3. Inspect required fields and conditions, including price, account/membership, reciprocal links, assets, review process, distribution and link type. For communities, check current promotion/link rules and whether the specific thread permits a relevant contribution and remains open. Read-only research does not authorize a submission or reply.
4. Check at least one recent comparable live placement to corroborate the route. For a new program with no examples, leave it unverified until equivalent first-party evidence resolves the quality, value and availability questions. A visible form shows an available preparation route; it does not prove that delivery, acceptance or publication will succeed.
5. Record the observation time with timezone, original/final URLs, evidence excerpts or screenshots, any visible placement dates and the precise scope verified (for example, public submission form inspected; delivery untested). Recheck the rules and route immediately before any later submission or outreach. Expired, changed or inaccessible evidence removes qualification until resolved.

If login, payment, CAPTCHA, a bot challenge, a broken form or missing research tools prevent a required check, mark the candidate **unverified** and state the missing evidence. Use a permitted alternate public first-party route if it answers the same question; do not infer what is behind a gate, bypass challenges or create an account. An independently accessible official invitation may verify a pitch route even when an unrelated member dashboard is gated. Clearly record what remains gated.

## Decisions and saved evidence

Save the report under `marketing/research/promotion-opportunities/<dated-run>/REPORT.md` in the selected promotion workspace. For an unregistered request, return it in chat under the shared workflow's rules. Separate qualification from the link tracker's execution states (`todo`, `prepared`, `submitted`, `live`, `rejected`). Researching a qualified site never makes a placement submitted or live.

For every assessed candidate record:

- Canonical identity, opportunity type, exact placement/category and submission URLs; duplicate/listing check.
- Quality/relevance observations with sources; traffic and SEO routes marked `pass`, `fail` or `unknown`, with evidence and limits for each. At least one value route must pass, alongside quality and current availability.
- Current eligibility, form/invitation/community rules, costs/conditions, sample placement and observed link/indexing behavior; timestamped first-party availability evidence and sourced evidence for the benefit route.
- Decision: `qualified`, `rejected` or `unverified`, with reasons and any unresolved check. Only `qualified` entries appear in the recommended shortlist. Keep the others in a brief exclusion log, outside the actionable catalog.
- Proposed contribution, effort, destination, next action and linked task ID where applicable. Keep product-specific reports, contact details and drafts private; never put real task queues or account data into shared skill resources.

Rank qualified opportunities by explained audience fit, evidence strength, expected value and effort. Default to at most five recommendations and prepare only the best one or two kits within the existing directory budget. Prefer an empty shortlist to busywork. Write proposals from sourced product facts and the actual form requirements; reuse existing drafts and avoid duplicate submissions.

Queue at most three next actions through the existing board, linking the qualification evidence and a mandatory pre-action recheck. Do not queue a submission for a rejected or unverified candidate. Mark research artifacts as draft evidence with the existing [helpers](../../../docs/HELPERS.md); publication requires separate live verification. Follow the shared workflow for applicable exact-content/destination approval. Creating a skill, finding a prospect or preparing a kit authorizes no accounts, submissions, outreach, spending, publication, commit or push.

After an authorized placement, verify its actual live URL, product facts, outbound link and attributes. Record acceptance separately from indexing, referral visits, signups/conversions and any search-performance change. Preserve unknown results; neither a placement nor a ranking change by itself establishes causation. Reuse this evidence to improve later choices, without assuming that an old successful opportunity remains open.

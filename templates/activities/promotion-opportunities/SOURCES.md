# Source notes for opportunity research

These are discovery and policy references, not an approved site catalog. Reopen the current primary sources when using them. Every prospective destination must pass [the qualification procedure](WORKFLOW.md) for the selected product in the current session.

## Discovery source reviewed on 2026-10-30

- Grant O's [X article](https://x.com/2whazzuup/status/2112291036315709828), published 2026-10-19, advertised 724 mostly free opportunities. Its linked [Backlinkforme database](https://backlinkforme.com/backlinks) displayed 498 listings when reviewed. The difference was not reconciled; neither figure represents verified usable placements.
- The article mixes directories, editorial prospects, communities, self-publishing and profiles. Duplicate destinations and misleading categories require independent classification. The database's Google Business Profile entry advertised instant approval; current Google eligibility rules exclude online-only businesses. Do not copy these assertions into a product's qualification record.
- Retain these links as starting points only. No listed site, price, link attribute, traffic metric or submission route is preapproved. No bulk list was imported. The article supplies no evidence that a placement will drive traffic or improve rankings for a particular product.

## Primary guidance

- [Google link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam): assess proposed link practices against the current policy, including low-quality directories, manipulative exchanges and paid ranking-credit offers.
- [Google outbound-link qualifications](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links): interpret `sponsored`, `ugc` and `nofollow` accurately; do not promise ranking credit.
- [Google Business Profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036): check business-model and in-person-service eligibility against the actual product before proposing a profile.

Official destination-specific submission pages, community rules and current comparable placements are required evidence. Third-party lists, traffic tools and search snippets cannot override them. Follow the shared [domain lookup](../domain-rank.md) for dated supporting metrics; unavailable figures stay unknown.

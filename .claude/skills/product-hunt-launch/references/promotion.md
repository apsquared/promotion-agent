# Promoting the launch

From Product Hunt's [How to promote your launch (and what to avoid)](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734384-5-how-to-promote-your-launch-and-what-to-avoid), checked 2026-11-09, plus founder accounts collected in Nevo David's playbooks (cited as their claims, not expected results).

## The rule

Ask people to **try the product, comment and share feedback**. Never ask for upvotes, and never run a giveaway or offer that requires or rewards one. When unsure whether something crosses the line, the user can ask hello@producthunt.com before acting.

Rewrite on sight:

| Don't write | Write instead |
| --- | --- |
| "Please upvote us on Product Hunt!" | "We launched on Product Hunt today. I'd love your honest take on it." |
| "Support us with an upvote 🙏" | "If you've used X, what would you tell a first-time user?" |
| "Upvote and comment to win a free year" | "Everyone from Product Hunt gets 30% off the first year." |
| "We need 500 upvotes to win" | "Here's what we built and why. Questions welcome all day." |

## Not allowed

Paid upvotes or comments, upvote-exchange groups, extra or fake accounts, posting from a company account, mass DMs, scraped email lists, copy-pasted messages to strangers, and posting the link in communities where the team has never taken part. Product Hunt removes launches and bans accounts for paid engagement; a sudden drop on the day often means it removed suspicious votes.

## Before launch day (weeks ahead)

- **Be a member first.** The maker comments on other launches, answers in the Forums and helps other makers, weeks before. Votes from established, active members count most, and this is how a new account becomes one.
- **List who already cares**: current users and signups, the newsletter, the team's own networks, and the communities (Slack, Discord, subreddits, forums) where the team is already active. Note each channel's rules about self-promotion.
- **Prepare the website**: a Product Hunt badge or embed from the pre-launch dashboard, optionally a landing page for Product Hunt visitors with one call to action, and the launch offer shown clearly.
- **Pre-launch dashboard** (website, the user's step): the launch link, the badge and a Forums thread announcing the launch.
- **Draft everything now**: email, social posts, community posts, in-app banner, a short note to each co-maker. One message per channel, written for that channel.

## On the day

- Send in the planned order once the launch is live (12:01 AM Pacific): team and co-makers first, then users and email, then social and communities.
- **One central post.** Papermark's founder said they put all promotion behind one tweet linking to the launch, and asked people to look at the tweet rather than to upvote; he reported close to 100,000 views and Product of the Day. A post people can read, reply to and reshare works better than a bare link.
- **Personal messages go to people the sender already knows.** Cold DMs land in requests and are often seen after the day ends.
- **Use the product's own surfaces**: an in-app banner, the README for open source, the docs site. Postiz's founder put a Product Hunt call to action in the README and estimated it brought close to 30 upvotes. His asked for upvotes; word yours as an invitation to try the product and comment.
- **Reply to every comment, fast.** A short demo video or a live link answers better than a long paragraph. Thank people, answer hard questions honestly, explain what is different and what you learned.
- Watch the launch dashboard. Third-party trackers such as Hunted Space show rankings before the main feed updates; treat them as unofficial.

## Founder accounts worth knowing

- Digger (12 Product Hunt launches in 18 months) found the launch depends on an audience outside Product Hunt: Slack and Discord communities, the founders' LinkedIn, Twitter, their own users and founder WhatsApp groups. Those outside people converted.
- KeepHQ reached Product of the Day without a hunter, with a complete product explained through familiar tools, and messaged communities that would relate plus people who had signed up from Hacker News.
- mirrord's team, by their own account, posted everywhere, asked friends to upvote and used untested messaging, and finished #14; they would have delayed until better prepared.
- Postiz led from the first hour with an unusual launch video, one X post and one LinkedIn post featuring it, messages in Slack groups where the team was active, and an open-source release the same day.
- Product Hunt, Hacker News and Reddit launches can be staggered over weeks or run together; both have worked.

## After

Thank supporters publicly and privately, share the result and what you learned, add any award badge Product Hunt emails, ask happy users for reviews, and post updates in the Forums between launches.
